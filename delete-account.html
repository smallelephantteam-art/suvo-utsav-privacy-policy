Suvo Utsav Account & Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Suvo Utsav account directly from inside the app.

  • Open Suvo Utsav.
  • Go to Profile.
  • Open Settings.
  • Tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion request.
Important:

Deleting your account permanently removes your account and associated personal data from Suvo Utsav. This action cannot be undone.
